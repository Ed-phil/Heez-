About Mas-Shiakt Nigeria Limited

Established 2015 | RC: 1269129 | Abuja, Nigeria

Our Story

Mas-Shiakt Nigeria Limited was incorporated in 2015 with a singular focus: to deliver practical agricultural and agribusiness solutions that transform the lives of smallholder women and youth farmers.

We are dedicated to interconnecting agricultural enterprises and providing high-quality training in best agronomic practices, financial literacy, group formation, processing, and market linkages. Our approach has enabled thousands of rural households to transition from subsistence farming to sustainable, profitable enterprises.

Our journey is rooted in a passionate commitment to four key Sustainable Development Goals: No Poverty, Zero Hunger, Good Health and Well-being, and Decent Work & Economic Growth. Every project, every partnership, and every decision reflects our dedication to inclusivity and positive environmental and social impact.

🤝

Our Commitment

We are committed to inclusive development that respects environmental boundaries and social responsibility. Our interventions emphasize regenerative agriculture, climate-smart practices, and digital technologies to help farmers establish revenue-generating platforms, create employment, and diversify household incomes.

Our Services & Focal Areas

Comprehensive solutions across the entire agricultural value chain

📚 Training & Capacity Building

Our cornerstone service focuses on knowledge creation and transfer, preparing smallholder farming communities with contemporary and sustainable agriculture practices for improved productivity.

What We Cover:

  • ✓ Best Agronomic Practices – Crop selection, soil management, water conservation, and seasonal planning
  • ✓ Group Formation & Development – Building cooperative structures, governance, and collective action
  • ✓ Farming as Enterprise – Business planning, record-keeping, and profit optimization
  • ✓ Cooperative Entrepreneurship – Value chain positioning and collective marketing
  • ✓ Financial Literacy – Savings, credit, investment, and risk management

Impact:

Beneficiaries gain the knowledge and confidence to adopt modern farming techniques, manage groups effectively, and position their farms as sustainable, income-generating businesses.

🌱 Extension Service Delivery

MSNL engages local youth as community extension workers, creating employment while ensuring practical, accessible, on-farm technical support.

Our Approach:

  • ✓ Extension Fundamentals Training – Community workers learn to diagnose problems and advise farmers
  • ✓ Crop-Specific Technical Knowledge – Deep expertise in maize, rice, ginger, and other value crops
  • ✓ Data Collection & Electronic Reporting – Real-time tracking of project outcomes and farmer progress
  • ✓ Blended Support – In-person visits, demonstration plots, and farmer field schools

Impact:

Farmers receive reliable, localized technical advice, while youth gain employment and professional development. Our partners like OCP Africa have recognized this model's effectiveness and continue to support its expansion.

🏭 Value Addition & Enterprise Development

We take pride in our comprehensive approach to post-harvest technologies and enterprise development, aimed at household income diversification and rural economic resilience.

Our Core Offerings:

  • ✓ Processing Technologies – Modular mills for maize and rice, traditional and improved techniques
  • ✓ Recipe Development & Food Safety – Improved recipes using household crops with certified food safety practices
  • ✓ Business Skills for Processors – Pricing, branding, bookkeeping, quality control, and customer management
  • ✓ Personal & Group Savings Programs – Building capital, credit access, and financial resilience
  • ✓ Market Linkages & Distribution – Connecting processors to retailers, institutions, and end markets

Recent Achievements:

17 Modular Processing Centers established across Kaduna, Nasarawa, and Benue, with trained women cooperatives managing operations, pricing, and quality.

">20+ Rice & Maize Enterprises now operating with emphasis on women and youth ownership, generating reliable household income.

🛒 Agro-Inputs Distribution & Services

As part of our operational mandate, we partner with local and international development agencies to ensure farmers have access to quality inputs at fair prices and convenient times.

What We Distribute:

  • ✓ Certified seeds (maize, rice, legumes, vegetables)
  • ✓ Fertilizers (NPK, urea, organic amendments)
  • ✓ Agrochemicals (pesticides, herbicides, fungicides)
  • ✓ Farm implements (hoes, cutlasses, rakes, plows)
  • ✓ Machines & tools (pumps, generators, processing equipment)

Our Advantage:

  • ✓ Quality Assurance – All inputs meet international and local standards
  • ✓ Bulk Procurement Power – Lower costs passed to farmers through group purchasing
  • ✓ Input Financing Programs – Partnership with OCP Africa and other lenders for farmer credit
  • ✓ Technical Guidance – Every input sale includes recommendations on proper use and timing

Scale of Operations:

Over the years, we have procured and distributed hundreds of tons of fertilizers (all major brands), seeds, agrochemicals, and farm implements to smallholder farmers, institutions, and organizations across multiple states.
